Article 4, answered

What is Article 4 of the EU AI Act?

Article 4 requires providers and deployers of AI systems to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among staff and other people operating AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education, training, and the context the AI system is used in.

When did Article 4 take effect?

2 February 2025. It's a Chapter I general provision under the Act's phased rollout (Article 113), applying alongside the Article 5 prohibited-practices ban — well before the bulk of high-risk obligations, which apply from 2 August 2026.

Who does Article 4 apply to?

Any organization acting as a provider or deployer of an AI system in the EU, regardless of size. There is no SME exemption from the literacy obligation itself.

We don't build AI — does Article 4 still apply to us?

Yes, if your staff use AI systems at all. The Act defines "deployer" broadly: if your team uses tools like Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise, or an AI-powered recruiting filter in the course of their work, your company is a deployer under the law — not just companies building their own models.

What counts as sufficient AI literacy?

There is no single fixed curriculum. The Act requires measures proportionate to each person's role, technical background, and the specific AI systems they use — which is why a generic course is a weaker compliance posture than a documented, role-aware training record.

Do we need to keep proof of training?

The Act doesn't prescribe a record format, but you must be able to demonstrate your measures if a national market surveillance authority asks. Every Litrix training record gets a permanent verification ID that can be shared with auditors and regulators — that's the practical answer.

What happens if we don't comply?

Article 4 falls under the Regulation's general obligations. Non-compliance with obligations of this kind can be penalized under Article 99(4) at up to €15 million or 3% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
