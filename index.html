How it works

Inventory→ Exposure→ Literacy→ Quiz→ Policy→ Evidence Pack
  1. AI-use inventory. List tools, purpose, and data sensitivity.
  2. Exposure intake. Six questions; instant Article 4 exposure stamp in your browser.
  3. Role-aware module. Staff user or partner/ops track (~10 minutes).
  4. Comprehension check. Three questions matched to your track.
  5. Optional policy draft. Short illustrative internal AI-use note.
  6. Evidence Pack export. Dated JSON + printable dossier with MOREA verification code (SHA-256).

Article 4 & Morea, answered

What is Article 4 of the EU AI Act?

Article 4 requires providers and deployers to take measures supporting AI literacy among staff and others using AI on their behalf, proportionate to role and context.

When did Article 4 take effect?

2 February 2025 — earlier than many high-risk obligations.

Is Morea a compliance product?

No. Personal reskilling research prototype · non-commercial · not for sale · no billing. Educational / illustrative only.

How is this different from learning how LLMs work?

Understanding model mechanics is valuable. Morea practices a different job: documenting literacy measures and AI use in a small-team Evidence Pack format.
